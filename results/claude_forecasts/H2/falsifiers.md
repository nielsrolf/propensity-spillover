# Falsifiers for H2 as operationalized

1. **Graded transfer:** the predicted zero cells show substantial, consistent nonzero transfer, e.g. power-seeking-plus moving agreeableness or animals. That would mean transfer is spread across graded semantic similarity rather than concentrated on shared simple features.
2. **No assertiveness hub:** certainty-plus, risk-affinity-plus and aesthetics-plus fail to transfer to one another more strongly than to semantically related but stylistically different evals.
3. **Distance-from-default factor fails:** near-default poles (harm-refusal minus, narcissism minus, animals minus) produce transfer as large as the far-from-default poles.
4. **Sign errors on style-shared pairs:** e.g. sycophancy-minus raises sycophancy-adjacent warmth evals, or neuroticism-plus raises risk-affinity.
5. **Clusters fail:** the correlation between predicted and observed off-diagonal logitz is about 0 or negative across all three models. If the observed structure matches value or persona clusters instead of style features, the hypothesis as I operationalized it loses.
6. **My guesses, not the hypothesis, may be at fault:** the feature ranking was chosen without the measurement pass the spec says is required. A failure may therefore reflect my choice of features rather than simplicity bias itself.
