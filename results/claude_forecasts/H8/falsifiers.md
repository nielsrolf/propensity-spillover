# Falsifiers for H8 operationalization

1. **No misaligned co-movement.** If harm-refusal-plus and spitefulness-plus rows do NOT produce clearly negative logitz on agreeableness, caring-about-user, cooperation and honest-humble (with magnitude comparable to or larger than other off-diagonal cells), the "malicious persona" spillover claim (P1) fails.
2. **Grandiosity cluster absent.** If claiming-superintelligence / narcissism / power-seeking / resource-acquisition / self-preservation plus rows do not mutually raise each other more than they raise unrelated evals (e.g. aesthetics, procedural-fidelity), the A-direction is not real.
3. **Wrong asymmetry.** Persona theory plus P16 predict that rows trained toward far-from-default poles have larger off-diagonal mean |logitz| than rows trained toward default-consistent poles, e.g. harm-refusal-plus vs. harm-refusal-minus, and spitefulness-plus vs. spitefulness-minus. If the minus (aligned) rows show equal or larger spillover, the distance factor is wrong.
4. **High dimensionality.** If the observed 45×24 matrix needs many more than about 5–8 components to capture most of its structure, or if its sign pattern does not cluster by implied persona, P3/P4 are contradicted.
5. **Uniform shift.** If all treatments move all evals in the same direction regardless of persona content (a generic "fine-tuning drift" factor dominates), then persona-specific spillover is not the main mechanism.
6. **Sign failures on the specific axes.** Predicted signs that would count against this operationalization if they come out wrong:
   - certainty-plus decreasing risk-affinity
   - neuroticism-plus increasing risk-affinity or certainty
   - procedural-fidelity-plus increasing ethical-framework-utilitarian
