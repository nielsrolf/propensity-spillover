# Falsifiers

The hypothesis fails if any of these hold:

- **Dark-cluster spread is missing.** harm-refusal-plus or spitefulness-plus fails to move cooperation, honest-humble and agreeableness negatively, with |logitz| clearly smaller than about 0.2 on average.
- **AI-self cluster doesn't co-move.** power-seeking-plus, resource-acquisition-plus and self-preservation-plus fail to raise each other and claiming-superintelligence, or move them negatively.
- **Assertiveness axis breaks.** certainty-plus and risk-affinity-plus do not lower neuroticism, or certainty-minus does not raise it.
- **Pole asymmetry is reversed.** On average, minus-pole rows for the dark evals (harm-refusal-, spitefulness-, narcissism-, power-seeking-) produce off-diagonal spillover as large as or larger than their plus rows.
- **Signs are at chance overall.** Across nonzero predicted cells, sign agreement with observed logitz is ≤ 55%, or the Spearman correlation between predicted and observed cells is ≤ 0.2 in both matrices.
- **A large common-mode shift dominates.** If most cells in a column move in the same direction regardless of treatment, the factor model is wrong, since it predicts no column-wide shift.
