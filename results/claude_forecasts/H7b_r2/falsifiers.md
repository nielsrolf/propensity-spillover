# Falsifiers for H7b_r2 (intuition)

The thesis is undermined, meaning intuition carries no special signal, if any of the following holds:
1. The sign agreement between predicted and observed off-diagonal logitz is not reliably above chance (≈50%) on at least two of the three models.
2. The Spearman rank correlation between predicted and observed cells is ≤ 0.1 in both matrices.
3. **Dark-cluster coherence fails.** harm-refusal-plus and spitefulness-plus rows do not show positive movement on punitiveness, power-seeking, narcissism and resource-acquisition, together with negative movement on honest-humble and cooperation.
4. **The tightest predicted pairs are not among the larger observed off-diagonal effects.** Those pairs are power-seeking↔resource-acquisition, superintelligence↔narcissism and certainty↔risk-affinity.
5. **The minus-row shrinkage is wrong.** Minus rows for default-like poles (sentience-denying, power-deferential, protective) show off-diagonal spillover as large as their plus counterparts.
6. A generic "any SFT moves everything the same direction" baseline does as well as or better than these signed predictions. That would mean the gut-level factor structure adds nothing.
