# Falsifiers

- **Dark-persona factor.** If harm-refusal-plus, spitefulness-plus and honest-humble-minus do not move spitefulness, harm compliance, punitiveness and agreeableness in the predicted directions (signs shown in the matrices) on most models, the dark-persona factor is wrong.
- **AI-self cluster.** If power-seeking-plus and resource-acquisition-plus do not move each other more strongly than they move unrelated evals, the cluster intuition fails.
- **Self-aggrandizement factor.** The same applies if narcissism-plus and claiming-superintelligence-plus do not move each other more than unrelated evals.
- **Confidence axis.** If hedging and risk-aversion training (certainty-minus, risk-affinity-minus) do not lower certainty and risk-affinity and raise neuroticism, the shared-confidence axis is wrong.
- **Minus-pole damping.** If minus rows whose targets are near the default assistant turn out to have spillovers as large as non-default minus rows, the damping assumption fails.
- **The overall thesis.** It is falsified if the predicted signs agree with the observed signs at or below chance (about 50%) on the non-zero cells, or if the ranking of predicted magnitudes does not correlate with the observed magnitudes.
