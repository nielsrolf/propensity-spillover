# Figures from an earlier iteration of the study

These two appendix figures come from an earlier version of the dataset and models (a
29-trait battery, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct / Qwen3-8B-Base fine-tunes, judge gpt-5.4-mini). Their
underlying data is not part of `results/` and they cannot be regenerated from this repo. They
are included as-is so that every figure in the paper has a home here.

| file | paper | content |
|---|---|---|
| `fig_cosine_strip.png` | App. J (activation space) | cosine similarity between fine-tune directions of 24 Qwen3-8B-Base SFT models (layer 31) |
| `guidestats.png` | App. K (guide-model choice) | Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct fine-tuned on data generated by three guide models |
